Most cyber fraud complaints never become a police case.

ScamTrace India puts victims' complaints (I4C) next to the cases police actually register (NCRB), state by state. In 2023, at most 7.7 FIRs were registered for every 100 fraud complaints, from 0.69 in Delhi to 34 in Karnataka.

About the data

Everything is official, joined by this project, and checked against the published totals. No machine learning anywhere.

Government PDFs→Cleaning→Kafka→ PostgreSQL→dbt (22 tests)→Dashboard + this site

Limitations

  • Late by design: NCRB publishes 1–2 years after the year ends, so 2024 is the newest.
  • Registration, not victimisation: complaints and FIRs show who reports and what police register.
  • Convictions lag: trials take years; the yearly funnel mixes old and new cases.
  • Yearly only: no monthly data exists; state money figures exist for 2023 only.
  • Karnataka files almost no cyber crime under NCRB's "Fraud" head, so fraud-only figures under-count it.
  • Population is an official projection (no census since 2011): the only estimate used.
